Who We Are

Justice starts with awareness. It becomes real through action.

I4J is a non-partisan, non-profit, youth-led movement committed to promoting justice, crime prevention, anti-corruption, accountability and good governance — operating across counties, schools, universities and communities.

Vision

A society where informed, empowered and responsible youth actively champion justice, accountability, good governance and social transformation.

Mission

Transforming awareness into action on justice, crime and governance issues through youth empowerment, mentorship, civic education, advocacy and community engagement.

Core Values

Justice, Integrity, Accountability, Youth Empowerment, Professionalism and Courage guide every program we run.

About Us

Built by young people who refused to stay silent

Why we were created

I4J was formed out of the urgent need to empower young people to become informed and responsible citizens — actively participating in crime prevention, anti-corruption efforts, good governance and social accountability, rather than watching from the sidelines.

Our objectives

We work to empower a generation of accountable youth, sustain mentorship in schools and community centers, build a national network of youth advocates, reduce risky behaviours such as crime, drug abuse and gender-based violence, and partner with institutions that share our mission.

Guiding principles

I4J upholds inclusivity, civic responsibility, evidence-based advocacy, community service, teamwork, non-discrimination and lawful conduct in everything it does — for members and the communities it serves alike.

Our identity

The Shield stands for protection and accountability. The Scales represent fairness and the pursuit of justice. The Arrow signals action, courage and forward movement — set in black, for discipline and credibility, and gold, for excellence and integrity.

Governance

How I4J is led and held accountable

I4J is governed under a written Constitution. Leaders are elected democratically and transparently every two years, and every office carries a defined mandate.

Chairperson

Provides strategic leadership, chairs meetings, represents I4J nationally, supervises leaders, approves partnerships and ensures accountability.

Vice Chairperson

Supports the Chairperson, coordinates departments, acts in the Chairperson's absence and ensures resolutions are implemented.

Secretary General

Maintains records, prepares agendas, records minutes, manages official communication and safeguards organizational documents.

Treasurer

Oversees finances, budgeting and fundraising accountability, banking, expenditure approval and financial reporting.

Project Manager

Designs, supervises and evaluates programs, coordinates outreach and mentorship activities and prepares implementation reports.

Media & Communication Director

Oversees branding, digital advocacy, public image, media engagement and social media campaigns.

Membership

Who can join

Membership is open to anyone who supports I4J's values and objectives, across four categories.

Category

Ordinary Members

Full members who vote, attend meetings and participate in activities.

Category

Student / School Members

Learners engaged through school-based mentorship and civic education.

Category

Volunteer Members

Members who commit time to outreach, events and campaign delivery.

Category

Honorary Members

Individuals recognized for exceptional support of I4J's mission.

Rights

Voice & Vote

Members vote, attend meetings, join activities and seek leadership positions.

Duty

Responsibility

Members uphold discipline, protect I4J's image and obey the Constitution.

Elections

Every two years

Leaders are elected democratically; candidates must show integrity, leadership and commitment to the mission.

Meetings

AGMs & special sessions

Annual General Meetings, Executive Meetings and Special Meetings, with quorum of at least half of registered members.

Finance

Transparent & audited

Funds from donations, grants, fees and partnerships; records kept transparently, with no funds misused for personal gain.

Partnerships

Schools, NGOs & government

I4J partners with institutions that strengthen justice reform, youth empowerment and advocacy work.

Conduct

Discipline & ethics

Corruption, abuse, discrimination or misuse of resources attract disciplinary action, including suspension or removal.

Evaluation

Monitoring & impact

Programs are tracked through data collection, reporting and regular assessment against our objectives.
